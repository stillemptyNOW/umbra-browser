Extensions

Unpacked Chrome extensions, loaded into every tab.

Restart Umbra for this change to take effect.

What actually works

Works. Content scripts, background pages and service workers, chrome.storage, chrome.runtime, chrome.tabs, chrome.i18n, and action popups. Manifest v2 and v3 both load.

Does not work. Request blocking — neither chrome.webRequest's blocking mode nor declarativeNetRequest is wired up in Electron. A content blocker installed here will load, show its interface, and block nothing. Umbra does that job itself, in the browser rather than in an extension.

Not accepted. Packed .crx files. Electron cannot load them, and unpacking an archive from the internet inside the browser's own process is a poor trade for the convenience. Extract the extension yourself and point Umbra at the folder.

Extensions run with the privileges their manifest asks for and can read every page you visit. Install the ones you would trust with your browsing history, because that is what you are doing.
